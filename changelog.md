# Dynamic version 1.1.0

The initial release of **Dynamic**—a simple, free, open-source yt-dlp GUI for Windows 10 and 11. Download video and audio from YouTube, YouTube Music, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, SoundCloud, and 1,000+ other supported sites.

## Features

- **Video & Audio Downloads** – Choose your preferred video resolution or switch to audio and select an MP3 bitrate, with an estimated file size shown before you download.
- **Precision Clip Trimming (TrimLoad)** – Preview the video in-app, set exact start and end timestamps, and download only the segment you need.
- **Live Downloads Tab** – Track real-time progress, download speed, ETA, and thumbnails, with plain-language error messages if something goes wrong.
- **Dark & Light Themes** – Automatically matches your Windows system theme or lets you pick your preference in Settings.
- **Built-in Updater** – Keep `yt-dlp`, `ffmpeg`, and Dynamic up to date from **Settings > Update** with incremental updates and scheduled helper checks.
- **System Tray Support** – Closing the main window keeps Dynamic running quietly in the system tray for quick access.
- **First-Run Setup Wizard** – Guided onboarding that walks through the terms and downloads `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg` automatically.
- **Clean & Private** – No ads, accounts, or analytics.
